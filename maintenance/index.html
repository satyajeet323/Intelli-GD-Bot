About The Product

A smarter way to practise discussions.

INTELLI BOT is a full-stack, AI-powered web application designed for students and professionals who want to practise and improve their Group Discussion skills. The platform creates a structured, technology-driven environment where users can participate in realistic discussions, communicate with other participants, receive AI-generated feedback and track their progress.

Instead of relying only on informal practice, INTELLI BOT combines Artificial Intelligence, real-time communication, quantitative scoring and peer evaluation to provide a complete GD preparation experience.

Practice Modes

Two ways to get better.

INTELLI BOT provides two complementary environments. Users can build confidence individually with AI and then test their skills in realistic multi-user discussions.

MODE 01

Individual AI Session

Practise alone against an intelligent AI discussion partner. The system generates a relevant topic, responds naturally to your arguments and evaluates your speaking performance.

This mode is designed to help improve confidence, vocabulary, fluency and structured thinking before entering a real group environment.

AI
MODE 02

Group Discussion Session

Join a shared virtual room with other authenticated participants. Each participant can communicate using video and audio while a real-time chat system enables text communication.

After the discussion, participants can evaluate one another and receive combined performance insights.
